Setup is a conversation

  1. Describe your business

    Tell Fundi what you sell, how you sell it and who works with you. Share a site you like and Fundi borrows its look.

  2. Check the proposal

    Fundi shows the pages, tables and fields it plans to create. Rename, add or remove anything before it is built.

  3. Go live and keep editing

    Your site and tools open on your own link. Ask Fundi for a change in plain words whenever the business changes.
